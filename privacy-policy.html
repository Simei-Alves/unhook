Privacy Policy

Unhook – Quit Smoking & Vaping
Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unhook – Quit Smoking & Vaping ("Unhook", "the app", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Unhook is designed to help users track and manage their progress toward quitting smoking and vaping.

Depending on the features you use, the app may allow you to enter information such as:

  • Your quit date or quit attempt date
  • Your previous smoking or vaping habits
  • The amount or frequency of smoking or vaping
  • Money spent on cigarettes or vaping products
  • Personal goals and progress information
  • Relapses, cravings, or other information you choose to record

This information is used to provide the app's features and calculate your progress, statistics, savings, and other related information.

2. Data Storage

Information entered into Unhook may be stored locally on your device. We do not intentionally collect or store this information on our own servers unless explicitly stated otherwise within the app.

If you uninstall the app or delete its local data, information stored locally may be permanently deleted.

3. Analytics and Third-Party Services

Unhook may use third-party services to help us understand app usage, improve performance, provide essential functionality, or display advertisements.

These services may collect certain technical information, such as device information, app interactions, diagnostic information, approximate location, advertising identifiers, or other information permitted by their respective privacy policies.

Third-party services used by the app may include advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or other service providers.

These third parties process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. Advertising

If advertisements are displayed in Unhook, advertising providers may collect information necessary to serve and measure advertisements, including advertising identifiers and information about interactions with ads.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate consent mechanisms may be provided before personalized advertising is enabled.

5. Health-Related Information

Unhook may allow you to record information related to your smoking or vaping habits and your quitting progress.

This information is intended solely to provide the functionality of the app. Unhook does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

Important: Unhook is a support and tracking tool and should not be considered a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited technical information with third-party service providers when necessary to operate the app, provide analytics, display advertisements, prevent abuse, diagnose crashes, or comply with legal obligations.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Unhook is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your information.

If information is stored locally on your device, you can generally remove it by using the relevant options within the app or by deleting the app's data or uninstalling the application.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
