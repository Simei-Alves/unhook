Privacy Policy

Unhook – Quit Smoking & Vaping
Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Unhook – Quit Smoking & Vaping ("Unhook", "the app", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Unhook is designed to help users track their progress when quitting smoking or vaping.

Depending on the features you use, you may voluntarily enter information such as:

  • Your quit date or quit attempt date
  • Your previous smoking or vaping habits
  • The amount or frequency of smoking or vaping
  • Money spent on cigarettes or vaping products
  • Personal goals and progress information
  • Relapses, cravings, or other information you choose to record

This information is used to provide the app's features, including progress tracking, statistics, milestones, cravings tracking, and estimated savings.

2. How Your Health-Related Information Is Stored

Information that you voluntarily enter into Unhook, including information about smoking, vaping, cravings, relapses, and quitting progress, is stored locally on your device unless the app explicitly states otherwise.

We do not intentionally upload or store this user-entered information on our own servers.

We do not use your smoking, vaping, craving, relapse, or quitting-progress information for advertising, advertising personalization, or behavioral advertising.

3. Data Retention

Unhook does not have a server-side retention period for user-entered health-related information because we do not intentionally store this information on our own servers.

Information stored locally on your device is retained for as long as the information remains stored by the application on your device.

You can remove locally stored information by using any available deletion or reset functionality within the app, by clearing the app's storage/data through your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

When local app data is deleted or the application is uninstalled, locally stored information is deleted from the device, subject to the operating system's normal behavior and any device-level backup services that may independently retain application data.

If a third-party service processes technical information on our behalf, that information may be retained by the third-party provider according to its own privacy policy, terms, and applicable retention practices.

4. Technical Information and Third-Party Services

Unhook may use third-party services to provide essential functionality, understand general app usage, diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse, improve app performance, or display advertisements.

Depending on which services are enabled in the app, these providers may process technical or device-related information such as:

  • Device model and operating system information
  • App version and installation information
  • IP address or approximate location derived from an IP address
  • Advertising identifiers
  • App interactions and usage information
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information
  • Information necessary to deliver and measure advertisements

Third-party providers process information according to their own privacy policies, terms, and applicable data-retention practices.

Third-party services may retain technical information for different periods depending on the purpose for which it was collected, their contractual obligations, legal requirements, security needs, and their own retention policies.

5. Advertising

If advertisements are displayed in Unhook, advertising providers may process technical information and advertising identifiers necessary to deliver, measure, and protect advertising services.

Advertising providers may retain advertising-related information according to their own privacy policies and retention practices.

Your voluntarily entered smoking, vaping, craving, relapse, and quitting-progress information is not intentionally shared with advertising providers for personalized advertising.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate consent mechanisms may be provided before personalized advertising is enabled.

6. Health-Related Information

Unhook allows users to record information related to smoking, vaping, cravings, relapses, quitting attempts, and quitting progress.

This information is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user, such as tracking progress, calculating statistics, displaying milestones, and estimating money saved.

Unhook does not provide medical diagnosis, medical treatment, psychotherapy, or professional medical advice.

Important: Unhook is a support and tracking tool and should not be considered a substitute for advice, diagnosis, or treatment from a qualified healthcare professional.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not intentionally share your voluntarily entered smoking, vaping, craving, relapse, or quitting-progress information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties for advertising purposes.

Limited technical information may be processed by third-party service providers when necessary to operate the application, provide analytics, display advertisements, diagnose crashes, maintain security, prevent abuse, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because user-entered quitting and progress information is stored locally on the device, the security of that information also depends on the security of the user's device, operating system, and device access controls.

9. Children's Privacy

Unhook is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your information.

Because information entered into Unhook is primarily stored locally on your device, you can generally exercise control over that information directly through the app or your device settings.

If you have a privacy-related request concerning information processed by us or our service providers, you may contact us using the email address below.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
